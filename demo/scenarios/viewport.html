Viewport entry (lazy loading)

Three <ad-unit loading="lazy"> elements below the fold. Each has a wide fetch-margin (auction starts while still offscreen) and a tighter render-margin (creative paints as the slot nears view). Scroll down and watch the event log on the right — ad-unit:fetch fires first (far zone), then ad-unit:render (near zone).

How lazy loading works

When loading="lazy", the component attaches built-in waitUntil(viewport-zone-promise) gates:

fetch zone
ad-unit:connected waits until the element intersects the fetch zone (configured by fetch-margin) before dispatching ad-unit:fetch.
render zone
ad-unit:fetch waits until the element intersects the render zone (configured by render-margin) before dispatching ad-unit:render.

The mock adapter is unchanged from eager mode: it hooks ad-unit:fetch to paint "auctioning…" then ad-unit:render to paint the winning bid. Lazy gating is purely a component concern.
