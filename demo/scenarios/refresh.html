refresh() — re-auction a live unit

unit.refresh() kicks off a new lifecycle cycle: ad-unit:refresh → ad-unit:fetch → ad-unit:render. The same waitUntil machinery used on initial connect applies. The mock adapter re-runs its fake auction and repaints the placard with a fresh bid price. The refreshCount property increments before each ad-unit:refresh dispatches and rides on every lifecycle event's detail.refreshCount.

What to watch in the log

Each refresh emits three events in order: ad-unit:refresh (new cycle starts; detail carries incremented refreshCount), then ad-unit:fetch (adapter starts auction), then ad-unit:render (adapter paints new bid). Burst refresh aborts any in-flight cycle via a cycle-id stale check — no duplicate render events.
